# Compliance FAQ

## Does registration prove compliance?

No. It provides ownership, scope and lifecycle evidence. The institution owns legal
interpretation, operating effectiveness and regulator crosswalks.

## Where does durable audit belong?

`agent-observability` owns cross-service audit. `agent-registry` remains the metadata registry and does not duplicate an audit
store.

## What should an adopter retain?

Retain approved cards, ownership evidence, S2S policy, lifecycle changes, deployment plans,
backup/restore results and the institution-owned regulatory mapping.
